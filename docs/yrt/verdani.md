# The Verdani

The Verdani are not an ancient species. They are a post-Fall phenomenon — ordinary people caught in the wrong place when containment failed.

## Origin

When the Fall disrupted the infrastructure maintaining green mana reservoirs, the failure was not clean. Boundaries collapsed gradually, over years and decades. The people still present when that happened — farm workers, groundskeepers, estate gardeners, the kind of person who simply loved living things and stayed close to them — absorbed sustained Verdant exposure at levels no one had planned for. The changes were slow, heritable, and permanent. The Verdani are their descendants: people whose biology has been reshaped by green mana deeply enough that the reshaping now propagates.

## The Pull

Verdani congregate around surviving green mana sources the way people congregate around warmth. It is not purely instinct, but it is not entirely choice either. Their physiology runs better near Verdant flow. Without it they become slow, grey, diminished — functional, but reduced. The bio-preserves, the old estate grounds, the patches of cultivated wilderness that survived the Fall: these are not just territory to the Verdani. They are the places where they can be fully themselves.

## Amber at the Fringe

By necessity, the Verdani have become expert readers of blight zone edges — not because they seek the contamination, but because the fringes are where Amber mana pools in useful concentrations. The same geographic instability that makes a blight boundary dangerous makes it productive for a people whose craft depends on Amber-laced materials. Verdani mask-carvers source their hardwood from trees that grew at these fringes, where Amber seeps into the grain over decades. They go carefully and they do not stay long, but they go.

## Animal Companions

Verdani have a deep affinity for animal companions, and most keep at least one. The relationship is not ownership in any sense they would recognise — it is closer to partnership, with the animal understood as a fellow creature of the ecosystem rather than a subordinate.

To strengthen and sustain these bonds, Verdani craftspeople shape collars, harnesses, and bands from the same Amber-laced fringe-wood used in mask-carving. Worn against the animal's skin, these objects carry a gentle Amber attunement that allows the Verdani to communicate intent, share attention, and offer limited direction — not compulsion, not override, but something closer to a whispered suggestion that the animal is already inclined to hear. A well-bonded companion will hold a position, warn of approach, or follow complex guidance across terrain without verbal command. The limits are real: the collar cannot make an animal act against its nature, and a frightened or injured companion will break the attunement entirely.

## Craft and Magic

Verdani magic does not come from Conclave training. There are no formal academies, no licensed disciplines, no standardised progression from apprentice to journeyman to master. What the Verdani have instead is accumulated tribal lore — knowledge passed down through family lines and community elders, tested against experience, revised when it fails, and held collectively rather than by any individual or institution.

This makes their practice look like intuition from the outside, and sometimes it is. A Verdani elder adjusting the Amber grain in a collar blank, or reading the Verdant flow at a blight fringe to judge how far is safe to go, is drawing on a body of knowledge built from generations of careful experiment rather than formal theory. The Conclave finds this deeply uncomfortable. The knowledge is real and demonstrably effective; it simply cannot be mapped onto the frameworks the Conclave uses to license and control mana practice. Verdani craftspeople are not registered. Their collars and masks are not certified. By Conclave standards, everything they do is unlicensed. The Verdani are aware of this and largely indifferent to it.

## The Touched

Mana exposure that would unsettle most communities is unremarkable among the Verdani. They themselves arose from contamination, and they live close enough to active Verdant flow and blight fringes that further change is simply the texture of existence. Touched individuals — those visibly or functionally altered by any mana — are welcomed without condition. To the Verdani this is not tolerance. It is theology: the Wildens hold that the Fall and what it did to human bodies was not catastrophe but judgment, and that the beast-marked, the changed, and the contaminated are not aberrations but the world's truest inheritors. Many Verdani are touched themselves. It is part of existing in harmony with the ecosystem rather than apart from it.

## Faith

The Verdani are adherents of the Wildens — an underground faith, persecuted by those who hold to human purity, centred on the Twelve Feral Gods and the belief that YRT's judgment expressed itself through mana's transformation of living things. The Wildens are pantheistic; they honour all twelve. But the Verdani have a particular closeness to **Koya**, the Red Stag-Mother — goddess of forests and wild places, of guardianship, of safe passage through danger, of the protection of the vulnerable. Her hooves leave no mark on soft earth unless she wills it. Where she rests, groves spring up behind her. The Verdani recognise themselves in that description.

The faith is not proselytising and the Verdani do not explain it to outsiders unless asked. It shows in how they act: in the care taken at a blight fringe, in the way a walker accepts the Mask-Risen obligation as service rather than sacrifice, in the long patience with which they tend a preserve that may outlast everyone currently alive on it. The briar wreath in Wildens iconography — thorned, entwined, enduring — could have been drawn from a Verdani preserve boundary. Perhaps it was.

## Mask-Risen as Policy

The practice is widely misunderstood. A Mask Risen is not a weapon of war. It is a boundary enforcement tool — the Verdani equivalent of a fence and a locked gate, deployed when the fence has been ignored too many times. The mask carries the recognition pattern for a specific threat; the walker carries the obligation until they die; the Verdant flow does the physical work. The Conclave objects not because the Mask-Risen is dangerous (it is rarely aimed at Conclave interests) but because it represents an independent mana discipline they do not license, control, or fully understand.

A Greatmask is a different statement. It means the Verdani have decided that a threat is systemic, and that the response needs to outlast anyone currently alive. Finding the keeper line and persuading them to stand it down is a negotiation with people who have already concluded that diplomacy failed.

## Disposition

The Verdani are not hostile by default. They are careful, patient, and very good at waiting. They will trade with outsiders, permit passage through managed land, and sometimes offer expertise to communities trying to restore damaged green mana infrastructure. What they will not do is cede ground. Every parcel they hold is the last of something. They know it. That knowledge sits just behind every conversation.
